    KNOW THE WARNING SIGNS

    How PhishGuard works

    Multiple signals help explain why an email may be risky.

    01

    Header analysis

    Checks sender and Reply-To domain mismatches, plus suspicious display-name claims.

    02

    Link inspection

    Looks for IP-based links, URL shorteners, lookalike domains and insecure login links.

    03

    AI-assisted detection

    When connected to the Flask backend and a compatible model, a TF-IDF and Logistic Regression model adds a phishing probability estimate.

    04

    Message signals

    Highlights urgency, sensitive-data requests, risky attachment types and common misspellings.

    Understand the verdict

    SafeFew known indicators were detected.
    SuspiciousSome warning signs need your attention.
    DangerousSeveral strong indicators or a high model score were found.

    This tool is for educational and defensive use. Do not open attachments or follow links simply because a scan labels an email safe.
